How it works

The server detects the sparkle logo in the corner, builds a mask, and inpaints only those pixels. Video reuses one mask across all frames (no flicker) and keeps the original frame rate, bitrate and audio.

Honest limits

This removes the visible logo only. Google's invisible SynthID pattern is embedded in the pixels themselves and is not removed. Use on content you made / own.
